Before Getting Started.

How much will a session cost?

Costs depend on insurance benefits and the plan. Intake will check coverage and discuss expected costs.

How soon can sessions begin?

Availability varies. The intake coordinator will discuss current openings and next steps.

Are evening sessions available?

Share scheduling preferences in the intake process. The coordinator will check available options.

Can I meet a therapist online?

Yes. Video sessions are available for people located in California.

What if my plan isn't accepted?

Intake will confirm whether Family Spring can work with the plan and explain the available next steps.
